06/SELECTED_RESEARCH_SYSTEMS
08 SYSTEMS
SMASHReview
Bit-Level Speech Faults

Framework probing speech-recognition robustness via sparse, semantically targeted parameter bit flips — controlled output changes, reliability, transferability and collateral effects. Under review at NeurIPS.

ACTAccepted
Trust Estimation

Anchor-calibrated trust estimation for recovering trusted and corrupted source cohorts when agreement-based methods become directionally ambiguous under corrupted majorities; targets binary cohort recovery. Accepted at IEEE CARS 2026.

HARPReview
Multi-Agent AI Safety

Studies how malicious or compromised agents in multi-agent LLM systems propagate local failures into system-level harm, and how that harm amplifies relative to a single agent. Under review at IEEE S&P.

DASHPublished
Adversarial ML / Meta-Attack

Meta-attack framework composing existing attack primitives to synthesize adversarial examples that are both effective and stealthy, rather than optimizing either property alone. Published at CVPR 2026.

BLADEReview
Bit-Level VLM Faults

Differentiable bit-level fault-analysis for quantized vision-language models, studying controlled semantic steering under sparse parameter faults. Evaluated on BLIP, BLIP-2 and GIT; basis of US Provisional 63/930,131 and an IEEE TIFS submission.

COBRAPublished
RLHF Integrity

Consensus-based reward pipeline that negates malicious human-in-the-loop feedback during RLHF, aggregating across a mixed-trust expert pool instead of rejecting updates outright. Published in Nature Scientific Reports; PCT filed.

Adversarial Eval Tooling
Internal Systems

Full-stack experimental tooling for structured review and downstream analysis of adversarial studies — repeatable AI evaluation workflows via a Streamlit interface and Python export pipelines: structured scoring, diff-based review, CSV export.

Edge LLM Inference
Heterogeneous Hardware

Prototype for resource-aware LLM execution across heterogeneous NVIDIA hardware, partitioning models layer-by-layer across Jetson Orin Nano, Xavier NX, AGX Orin and an RTX A6000, with models up to the 15B scale (StarCoder).
